## This stage: the roster table

The roster screen is generated by the slice generator from the approved spec, `specs/hero.json`, then finished by hand. The roster hosts the hero edit form in its dialogs, so generate the form and the roster together. Run exactly:

`npx ng g slice --spec specs/hero.json --kinds form,manage`

It writes the hero form under `src/app/ui/hero-form/`, and replaces the placeholder in `src/app/heroes/` (the files marked `slice:placeholder`) with the roster screen: `heroes.ts`, `heroes.html`, `heroes.css`, `heroes.view-model.ts` and its spec. The screen has the filter bar with search and the filters the spec names, the roster table with the spec's columns, and the summary footer when the spec asks for one. The table sits in a scroll container whose own overflow scrolls, which is the build spec's answer (a) for the table at 375px. The route that already loads `./heroes/heroes` keeps its path, and the generator adds no second route.
